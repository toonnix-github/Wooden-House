# Book 2 Resolution: Echoes in the Wooden House

## 1. The Echo of Na Dao (Khin's Dream Sequence)
The resolution begins with Khin experiencing his most visceral dream yet. He isn’t just watching Na Dao; he *is* Na Dao. He feels the damp earth squelching beneath his bare feet, the suffocating humidity of a Thai monsoon night, and the terrifying, heavy thud of footsteps pursuing him. Through her eyes, he sees the dark silhouettes of men sent by Prof C. Khin's lungs burn with Na Dao's exhaustion. In her desperate final moments, she sprints into the old Wooden House. Her hands, trembling and muddy, pry up a loose teak floorboard near the *Sao Eak* (the main spirit pillar). She shoves a small, oilcloth-wrapped ledger into the dark cavity, desperately forcing the wood back into place. A second later, a rough hand grabs her hair, pulling her violently backward into the dark. Khin wakes up screaming, his own heart pounding against his ribs, phantom mud seemingly still clinging to his fingers.

## 2. The Unearthing
Driven by the fading adrenaline of the dream, Khin rushes to the real Wooden House in the early hours of the morning. He finds the exact floorboard near the main pillar. It’s stiff and warped by decades of humidity, but with a crowbar, he pries it up. Reaching into the dust and cobwebs, his fingers brush against aged oilcloth. He pulls out the ledger. It’s perfectly preserved—a relic of decades-old sins.

## 3. The Connecting Thread
Khin gathers Wi, Mind, and Oad. They carefully peel back the cloth to examine the ledger. It contains meticulously handwritten records of drug supply lines, payoff amounts to local police, and operational costs from the 90s. As Wi scans the faded ink, the blood drains from her face. She points to a recurring name: "Chaiwit" (Prof C's real name). 

Wi connects the dots: the low-level thugs blackmailing her with the social media photos (let's call them Bank and Tor) frequently boast on their private streams about working for "Ajarn C"—an untouchable boss. The blackmailers aren't just random creeps from campus; they are the modern, bottom-rung enforcers of the exact same syndicate that murdered Na Dao decades ago. 

## 4. The College-Kid Strategy
They are just university students, not detectives. Going to the corrupt local police is a death sentence, and they have no combat skills. They decide to play the only card they have: Mutually Assured Destruction. 
Oad, being the most tech-savvy, photographs and scans every page of the ledger. He uploads the files to a secure cloud server, setting up an automated "dead-man's switch." If Oad doesn't reset a timer every 24 hours, the ledger—exposing Prof C's foundational crimes, aliases, and historical money laundering—will be emailed simultaneously to major Thai investigative journalism pages (like *The Reporters* or *Isranews*), the national cyber-police division in Bangkok, and subtly leaked to rival syndicates.
To make the blackmailers back off, they have to present this threat directly to Bank and Tor.

## 5. The Confrontation and The Sister's Shield
They arrange to meet Bank and Tor at an abandoned chemistry annex on the edge of the university campus. The tension is palpable. Wi, trying to keep her voice from shaking, slides a printed envelope of the ledger copies across a rusted table. She explains the dead-man's switch: if the blackmail photos of her go online, Prof C's entire empire gets burned down, and Prof C will know exactly which two street-level idiots caused it.

Instead of backing down out of fear, Bank flies into a panicked rage. Realizing their boss will kill them if he finds out they caused this leak, Bank decides it's easier to beat the passwords out of the kids right now and destroy their phones. He pulls a heavy steel pipe from his jacket and lunges at Wi. Khin and Oad try to intervene but are easily thrown aside by the older, stronger thugs.

Bank corners Wi against the wall, raising the pipe. At that exact microsecond, the temperature in the room plummets. 

A heavy, metal folding chair sitting five meters away suddenly screeches across the concrete floor as if kicked by an invisible giant, slamming brutally into the back of Bank's knees. As Bank buckles in pain and confusion, the fluorescent lightbulb directly above him explodes, showering the thugs in sparks and shattered glass. 
*Va*, Wi's ghost sister, has manifested just enough energy in her protective fury to physically intervene. 

## 6. The Aftermath
The sudden, violent supernatural display terrifies Bank and Tor, who are deeply superstitious. Bleeding from the glass and terrified of the "angry spirit" protecting the girls, the thugs scramble out of the annex, leaving their weapons behind. 
Knowing the kids possess both a dead-man's switch that could destroy him and the literal backing of a vengeful ghost, Prof C (informed by his panicked thugs) orders his men to stand down. The blackmail photos are permanently deleted from their servers. The group is safe for now, but the line between the living and the dead has been irrevocably crossed, and the true scope of Prof C's evil sets the stage for Book 3.
