# Plot Resolution: Book 2 - The Ledger and the Trap

## 1. The Catalyst: Echoes of the Past
The turning point begins with Khin's supernatural connection to the wooden house. After days of relentless anxiety over the blackmail, Khin falls into a deep, feverish sleep and experiences a vivid dream. He sees the house decades ago, watching a shadowy figure (connected to Prof C's syndicate) prying up a specific, unnoticeable floorboard under the main staircase. 

Waking up, Khin leads the group (Wi, Mind, Oad) to the spot. They pry it open and discover a decaying, physical ledger. It’s Prof C’s original "insurance policy"—a detailed account of the drug ring's operations, payoffs, and the real names of the low-level enforcers. Among those names are "Benz" and his crew, the exact men currently blackmailing Wi with compromising social media material from her days as an abused runner.

## 2. The Strategy: Wi's Turf
While the others want to go straight to the police, Wi stops them. She knows Benz. The police are slow, and Benz would leak the photos the second he got wind of an investigation. Instead, Wi takes charge, her past transforming from a source of shame into their greatest weapon. 

Wi explains Benz's operating methods. They are arrogant, creatures of habit who still use the same drop-off points from five years ago. She knows they use burner phones but have a glaring weakness: they always do a pre-drop sweep at a specific late-night noodle stall near an abandoned river warehouse complex. 

The plan: Use the ledger as bait. Wi will contact Benz, claiming she found Prof C's master ledger and wants to trade it for them wiping the blackmail material permanently. In reality, they are setting a trap. 

## 3. The Setup: College Tech meets Street Smarts
They choose the river warehouse for the meet. Wi knows the layout intimately, including every blind spot and escape route. Oad uses his engineering skills to rig cheap, battery-powered webcams around the warehouse, streaming a live feed to Khin and Mind, who act as "overwatch" from a borrowed van parked blocks away. 

Wi insists on going in alone. Benz would smell a trap if he saw the others. She carries a fake ledger stuffed with blank pages, while Khin holds the real one, ready to send scanned copies to a ruthless investigative journalist if Wi gives the safe word.

## 4. The Climax: The Ghost in the Machine
Benz and two of his thugs arrive. The tension is palpable. Benz is cruel, immediately trying to revert to their old power dynamic, mocking Wi and threatening her. He pulls out his phone, his thumb hovering over the 'publish' button for the blackmail photos, demanding the ledger first.

Wi holds her ground, utilizing street-level psychological tactics. She stalls, pointing out that if he posts the photos, he loses his leverage and gets nothing but a target on his back. She speaks his language, cold and calculating. 

Enraged by her defiance, Benz loses his patience. Realizing she isn't the scared girl she used to be, he lunges at her with a switchblade. He's too fast; Wi has no room to dodge. 

**The Supernatural Assist:** At that exact critical second, a heavy, rusted iron hook hanging from an overhead ceiling crane violently snaps off its thick chain. It plummets down, crashing onto the concrete floor *precisely* between Benz and Wi, shattering the ground and sending shrapnel flying. Simultaneously, an unnatural, freezing wind sweeps through the muggy warehouse. 

It’s Va, Wi’s ghost sister. 

Benz stumbles backward in shock, his focus broken by the sheer impossibility of the falling hook. Wi doesn't hesitate. Seizing the distraction, she kicks Benz's knee, snatching the phone from his grip as he falls, and smashes it repeatedly under her boot until it's completely destroyed.

## 5. The Resolution: Checkmate
Benz's thugs rush forward, but Wi stands tall over the destroyed phone. She yells out to the hidden cameras. Oad's feed has recorded everything—Benz threatening her with a deadly weapon, and his arrogant admission of drug ties on tape. 

Wi informs Benz that the *real* ledger, along with the live video of his attempted murder, are on a dead-man's switch. If the blackmail photos ever surface anywhere, or if he ever comes near her friends again, the files will be sent directly not to the police, but to Prof C's rival cartel bosses—people who Benz fears far more than the law. 

Defeated, terrified of the "curse" of the warehouse, and outplayed by the girl he thought he owned, Benz and his crew flee into the night. 

The group regroups at the van. Wi looks back at the warehouse, whispering a silent "thank you" to Va. The college students realize they survived by combining their diverse skills, and Wi finally understands that her past doesn't define her as a victim; it forged her into a survivor.
