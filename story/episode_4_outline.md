# Episode 4: Whispers in the Faculty

## Overview
This episode covers the early-to-mid period of Term 1. It establishes the multi-thread mystery: the beginning of Mind's toxic friendship with Pim, the subtle oppression of the unofficial room-cheer system, and the first clear appearances of the Wet Bench Ghost and the Storage Room Ghost. The core theme highlights that the real horrors are the silent cruelty of the university system, not the supernatural entities.

---

## Scene 1: The "Perfect" Friend
**Location:** Faculty Library / Study Area
**Characters:** Khin, Mind, Pim, Odd (cameo)
**Plot Beats:**
- Mind and Pim are studying together. Pim presents herself as the perfect, supportive friend, praising Mind’s diligence and intellect.
- Feeling insecure about her scholarship, Mind is relieved to have Pim and willingly shares her detailed study summaries.
- Khin observes them from a nearby table. He notices Pim smoothly taking photos of Mind's notes when Mind turns away.
- Khin feels a subtle unease about Pim's overly sweet demeanor but dismisses it as him being cynical. 
**Atmosphere:** Bright and academic, appearing completely normal on the surface but underscored by a subtle, parasitic tension.

---

## Scene 2: The Wet Bench
**Location:** Corridor in front of the Teacher's Lounge
**Characters:** Khin, Mind, Pim, Teacher B, Wet Bench Ghost
**Plot Beats:**
- Khin and Mind walk past the Teacher's Lounge. Mind mentions wanting to consult Teacher B about a scholarship application.
- Khin notices a three-seater wooden bench outside the lounge. The rightmost seat is completely wet, despite there being no rain.
- Sitting there is a ghost in an old, faded student uniform. She blankly mutters: *"Is the teacher in?"* and *"Read it too."*
- Teacher B exits his office. He is overly charismatic and touches Mind’s shoulder a little too familiarly, making Khin uncomfortable. 
- As Teacher B interacts with Mind, the ghost's murmurs grow slightly more frantic.
- Pim suddenly arrives, interrupting the conversation and effortlessly drawing Teacher B's attention to herself, acting like she is protecting Mind.
**Atmosphere:** Chilly and uncomfortable. The contrast between Teacher B's forced warmth and the cold, static presence of the ghost creates a creeping sense of dread.

---

## Scene 3: Unseen Chains
**Location:** Faculty Courtyard (Dusk)
**Characters:** Khin, Odd, Senior Students, Quiet Kid, Room Cheer Ghost
**Plot Beats:**
- Khin and Odd are leaving late. They pass a group of seniors gathering first-years for an "unofficial" meeting. 
- The seniors are not yelling; they are politely coercive: *"You don't have to stay, but your friends will remember who was here for them."*
- Khin notices the "Quiet Kid" standing at the edge of the group, looking trapped and distressed.
- Standing among the seniors, unnoticed by the living, is the Room Cheer Ghost. It repeats past echoes: *"Louder," "Ready yet?," "Friends are waiting."*
- Khin realizes the ghost is a past student trapped in this cycle of pressure. Unable to intervene against the social norm, Khin walks away with Odd, feeling a heavy sense of guilt.
**Atmosphere:** Claustrophobic and oppressive. The fading light of dusk mirrors the suffocating social pressure that traps the students.

---

## Scene 4: The Closed Door
**Location:** Isolated Corridor near the Storage Room
**Characters:** Khin, Quiet Kid (distant), Storage Room Ghost
**Plot Beats:**
- Khin walks down a quiet, dusty corridor and hears a faint, rhythmic knocking coming from an old storage room.
- He sees a shadowy figure near the door, whispering repeatedly: *"Open... open please."*
- Thinking it might be a ghost, Khin hesitates. Down the hall, he spots the Quiet Kid hurrying away, looking terrified.
- Khin approaches the door, but the knocking stops. He realizes the sound might not have been just a ghost, but an echo of real bullying—someone might have actually been locked inside earlier, and he arrived too late to help.
**Atmosphere:** Eerie, isolated, and thick with regret. The horror comes from Khin's realization of his own inaction in the face of human cruelty.

---

## Scene 5: The Blowback
**Location:** The Wooden House (Night)
**Characters:** Khin, Na Dao (Ghost)
**Plot Beats:**
- Khin returns to the Wooden House, physically and mentally drained by the day's heavy atmosphere.
- The house feels unusually cold, reacting to Khin's stress. Na Dao's presence is felt as a glass of water slides gently across the table toward him.
- Khin speaks aloud to the empty room, venting about the suffocating environment, the ghosts that only repeat themselves, and the people who pretend to be kind.
- His phone buzzes. In the faculty LINE group, an anonymous message drops a vague hint about someone "getting close" to Teacher B for grades—the first seed of Pim's sabotage against Mind.
- The lights in the house flicker slightly. Na Dao remains silent, a comforting but powerless presence against the human malice brewing outside.
**Atmosphere:** A melancholic safe haven. Quiet comfort mixed with a looming anxiety as the outside world threatens to close in.
