# Episode 6 Outline: ปลายเทอม 1 (Late Term 1)

**Theme:** The spread of malice, the cost of inaction, and the Blowback Effect on the wooden house.
**Focus:** Mind's sabotage, the worsening rumors, the Quiet Kid's absence, and the evolving supernatural phenomena.

## Scene 1: The Blowback Effect
* **Location:** The Wooden House (Morning)
* **Characters:** Khin, Na Dao (presence)
* **Plot Beats:** 
  * Khin wakes up to find the house noticeably colder and gloomier. A small potted plant near the window has started to wilt.
  * Na Dao’s presence feels fainter than usual. 
  * Khin realizes that the mounting stress and toxic environment at the university are causing a "Blowback Effect," draining the house's energy.
* **Atmosphere:** Quiet, melancholy, ominously chilly.

## Scene 2: The Poisonous Whisper
* **Location:** Faculty Canteen / Hallway
* **Characters:** Khin, Mind, Pim, Odd, Wi, background students
* **Plot Beats:** 
  * Rumors about Mind secretly seeing Teacher B for grades are loud enough to hear from passing tables. 
  * Pim loudly and artificially defends Mind against a group of gossiping students, making sure everyone hears her "defense," which only draws more unwanted attention.
  * Mind looks completely exhausted and drained. She excuses herself to prepare for her crucial scholarship interview.
  * Wi is fiercely skeptical of the rumors, while Odd is cluelessly loud. Khin notices a subtle, satisfied smirk on Pim's face as Mind walks away.
* **Atmosphere:** Suffocating, paranoid, laced with fake sympathy.

## Scene 3: The Paralyzing Fear and the Wet Stain
* **Location:** Corridor outside Teacher B's Office
* **Characters:** Khin, Mind, The Wet Chair Ghost
* **Plot Beats:** 
  * Khin walks down the hall and spots Mind standing a few meters away from Teacher B's door. She is holding her assignment, desperate to consult the teacher to fix her dropping grades.
  * However, she hears a student whispering nearby. Paralyzed by the fear of the rumors, Mind turns back, sacrificing her grades to avoid being seen with Teacher B.
  * As Khin passes the 3-seater bench, he sees the female student ghost sitting there. The puddle of water is significantly larger today, dripping heavily onto the floor—a reflection of Mind's intense stress and the toxic rumors involving Teacher B.
  * The ghost weakly mutters, "Read it..."
* **Atmosphere:** Oppressive, damp, tragic.

## Scene 4: The Missing Boy & The Echoes of Bullying
* **Location:** Cheer Room / Storage Room Hallway
* **Characters:** Khin, Odd, Senior Students, Storage Room Ghost (audio)
* **Plot Beats:** 
  * During a late-evening cheer session, the seniors' pressure feels heavier. Khin scans the room and realizes the "Quiet Kid" is completely absent.
  * Odd whispers that the kid hasn't been to class for a few days, but nobody—neither the seniors nor the peers—seems to care. They are just letting him disappear from the system.
  * Later, walking past the Storage Room, Khin hears faint scratching and knocking. A senior walks by and jokes, "Ghost is active today," completely oblivious to the fact that real students were once locked in there. Khin feels the lingering echo of a real person's despair.
* **Atmosphere:** Tense, claustrophobic, uncaring.

## Scene 5: The Sabotage
* **Location:** Student Lounge (Morning of the Scholarship Interview)
* **Characters:** Khin, Mind, Pim, Wi
* **Plot Beats:** 
  * Mind is frantic—her portfolio and required documents for the scholarship interview are missing from her bag.
  * Pim is "frantically" helping her search but keeps making counterproductive suggestions, like telling Mind she probably left them at her dorm and should run back.
  * Khin and Wi try to help. Wi tells Mind to just go to the interview and explain the situation to the committee, but Mind is spiraling into a panic attack.
  * Khin closely observes Pim and notices her eyes lack real panic; her movements are performative, effectively wasting Mind's precious time.
* **Atmosphere:** Frantic, stressful, heart-pounding.

## Scene 6: Too Late
* **Location:** Outside the Interview Room
* **Characters:** Khin, Mind, Pim
* **Plot Beats:** 
  * Mind finally runs to the interview room, but the door is firmly shut. 
  * The committee assistant informs her that she missed her slot and the committee has already moved on. Mind collapses against the wall in tears, realizing she has lost the funding she desperately needs.
  * Pim hugs her, playing the perfect, consoling best friend.
  * Watching from a distance, Khin feels a dark, sickening realization that Pim orchestrated this entirely. He realizes that human malice is far more terrifying and calculated than any ghost he has ever encountered.
* **Atmosphere:** Devastating, cruel, heavy.

## Scene 7: Unresolved Shadows
* **Location:** The Wooden House (Night)
* **Characters:** Khin, Na Dao (action)
* **Plot Beats:** 
  * Khin returns home, carrying the heavy weight of the unresolved day. Term 1 is ending on a dark note: Mind's grades are dropping, the scholarship is gone, the rumors are thriving, the Quiet Kid is missing, and the ghosts are still waiting.
  * He sits alone in the dark kitchen. The house feels cold, but suddenly, a glass of warm milk is gently pushed across the table toward him by an unseen force.
  * Khin looks up. He realizes he can't just be an observer anymore. The system is breaking his friends, and if he doesn't stand up to the human malice, it will eventually destroy everything, including this house.
* **Atmosphere:** Somber, reflective, with a lingering unresolved tension but a spark of new resolve.
